# Meaningful human oversight should be designed into the AI interface.

## Summary

The EU AI Act's Article 14 treats oversight as a system design requirement for high-risk AI, including human-machine interface tools that allow natural persons to oversee use.

## Scores

- Evidence strength: 84.0

## Source

- Title: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Artificial Intelligence Act - Article 14 Human Oversight
- Publisher: EUR-Lex
- Authors: European Parliament and Council of the European Union
- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj?locale=en
- Language: en

## Limitations

The regulation targets legally defined high-risk AI systems; consumer wellness or learning tools may not be in scope, but the design principle transfers well.
