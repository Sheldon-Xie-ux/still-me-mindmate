# GenAI changes critical thinking from creation toward verification and stewardship.

## Summary

The Microsoft Research paper reports that GenAI-assisted work shifts critical thinking toward checking information, integrating responses, and managing the task rather than only producing original reasoning.

## Scores

- Evidence strength: 82.0
- Relevance: 91.0
- Source credibility: 90.0

## Source

- Title: The Impact of Generative AI on Critical Thinking
- Publisher: Microsoft Research / CHI 2025
- Authors: Hao-Ping Lee, Advait Sarkar, Lev Tankelevitch, Ian Drosos, Sean Rintel, Richard Banks, Nicholas Wilson
- URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-impact-of-generative-ai-on-critical-thinking-self-reported-reductions-in-cognitive-effort-and-confidence-effects-from-a-survey-of-knowledge-workers/
- Language: en

## Limitations

The shift can be productive when tools support verification, but harmful if product design hides sources, uncertainty, or the user's initial reasoning step.
