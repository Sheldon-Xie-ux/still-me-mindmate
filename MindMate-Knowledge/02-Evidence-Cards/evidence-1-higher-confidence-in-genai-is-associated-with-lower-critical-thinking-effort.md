# Higher confidence in GenAI is associated with lower critical-thinking effort.

## Summary

In a CHI 2025 survey of knowledge workers, confidence in GenAI predicted less self-reported critical-thinking effort, while task-specific self-confidence predicted more critical thinking.

## Scores

- Evidence strength: 86.0
- Relevance: 94.0
- Source credibility: 90.0

## Source

- Title: The Impact of Generative AI on Critical Thinking
- Publisher: Microsoft Research / CHI 2025
- Authors: Hao-Ping Lee, Advait Sarkar, Lev Tankelevitch, Ian Drosos, Sean Rintel, Richard Banks, Nicholas Wilson
- URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-impact-of-generative-ai-on-critical-thinking-self-reported-reductions-in-cognitive-effort-and-confidence-effects-from-a-survey-of-knowledge-workers/
- Language: en

## Limitations

Survey and self-report evidence can reveal associations and lived examples, but it does not prove that GenAI causes durable cognitive decline.
