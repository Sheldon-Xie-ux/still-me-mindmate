# Education-facing GenAI needs age-appropriate, human-centred governance.

## Summary

UNESCO frames GenAI in education around human capacity, data privacy, age limits, ethical validation, and pedagogical design rather than unrestricted answer generation.

## Scores

- Evidence strength: 78.0
- Relevance: 90.0

## Limitations

Policy guidance is a strong design input, but it should not be treated as experimental proof that a specific interface improves learning outcomes.
