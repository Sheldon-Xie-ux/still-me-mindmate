# Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Artificial Intelligence Act - Article 14 Human Oversight

- Authors: European Parliament and Council of the European Union
- Publisher: EUR-Lex
- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj?locale=en
- Retrieved at: 2026-05-26 02:05:19.497327

## Snippet

Article 14 requires high-risk AI systems to be designed with human-machine interface tools that allow effective oversight by natural persons.
