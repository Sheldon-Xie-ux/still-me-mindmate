# The Impact of Generative AI on Critical Thinking

- Authors: Hao-Ping Lee, Advait Sarkar, Lev Tankelevitch, Ian Drosos, Sean Rintel, Richard Banks, Nicholas Wilson
- Publisher: Microsoft Research / CHI 2025
- URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-impact-of-generative-ai-on-critical-thinking-self-reported-reductions-in-cognitive-effort-and-confidence-effects-from-a-survey-of-knowledge-workers/
- Retrieved at: 2026-05-26 02:05:19.495891

## Snippet

A survey of 319 knowledge workers found that confidence in GenAI is associated with less self-reported critical-thinking effort, while self-confidence is associated with more critical thinking.
