# Google Effects on Memory: Cognitive Consequences of Having Information at Our Fingertips

- Authors: Betsy Sparrow, Jenny Liu, Daniel M. Wegner
- Publisher: Science
- URL: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1207745
- Retrieved at: 2026-05-26 02:05:19.497980

## Snippet

Four studies suggest people remember less information itself when they expect future access, and remember more about where to access it.
