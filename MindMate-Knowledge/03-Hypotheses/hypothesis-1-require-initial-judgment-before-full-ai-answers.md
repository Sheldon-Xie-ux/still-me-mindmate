# Require initial judgment before full AI answers

## Statement

For learning and high-stakes reasoning tasks, MindMate should ask users to state an initial judgment or criteria before revealing a complete AI recommendation.

## Rationale

Critical-thinking, oversight, and education-governance sources converge on the same product pattern: make the user's reasoning visible before the AI answer becomes the default frame.
